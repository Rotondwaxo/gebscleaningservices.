Our Story

Building Trust Through Quality Cleaning

Gebs Cleaning Services was established in 2018 by Gebrehiwot "Gebs" Tekle, a former facility manager with over 10 years of experience in the cleaning industry. After years of managing cleaning operations for large commercial buildings, Gebs saw an opportunity to bring professional-grade cleaning to residential properties and small businesses.

What started as a one-person operation has grown into a trusted team of 15 dedicated cleaning professionals serving over 200 regular clients across the region. Our growth has been driven by one simple principle: exceptional service that exceeds expectations every time.

Today, we're proud to be one of Cape Town's most trusted cleaning service providers, known for our attention to detail, reliability, and commitment to environmentally responsible practices.
